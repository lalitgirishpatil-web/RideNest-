About RideNest

RideNest is a student-focused ride booking website designed to make daily travel simple, convenient, and affordable for college students.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to help students explore convenient travel options for their daily journeys to college and other destinations.

Our Features

Affordable Travel

Ride options with different prices to suit student budgets.

Easy Booking

A simple form to enter pickup and destination details.

Driver Information

View simulated driver and vehicle details after booking.

Our Vision

We aim to create a simple and user-friendly platform that makes everyday student travel more convenient.
